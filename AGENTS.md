# PKSEI

Four drunk guys have lashed two aluminium canoes into a catamaran and need to reach an island in the middle of the lake. You control all four at once. The lake throws random hazards at them, and they keep drinking, so the boat gets harder to control the longer the crossing takes. Nobody dies: a bad stretch drifts the boat back into water already crossed. A top-down, bright pixel-art rage game in the spirit of Getting Over It, played for the comedy of watching four idiots fail together.

Instructions for AI assistants working on this game. The workflow is Godot Director: its rules are in `.godot-director/rules.md`, which names the procedure for each kind of request. Read it before any work, unless your tool has already loaded it (Claude Code imports it: @.godot-director/rules.md).

## Project facts
- Godot 4.7 · GDScript · 2D, top-down · Web (HTML5, itch.io); desktop builds for development
- Rendering and window: GL Compatibility · 640×360 viewport, `canvas_items` stretch, aspect `keep`, fractional scaling (integer scaling left phones with a 1× canvas: B6) · nearest texture filter, 2D transforms snapped to pixels

## Architecture
One row per system: what it owns and where its boundary is. Screens depend on systems, never the other way round.

| System | Owns | Where | Talks to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RunState` (autoload) | the current attempt: four `Guy`s (seat, drunkenness, stamina, aboard), progress 0..1 from shore to island, elapsed time, phase LAUNCH → CROSSING → WON; owns the `Paddling`, `Drunkenness`, `Stamina`, `Antics` and `Rescue` instances and one RNG; the hazard effects' state (pending shove and stop, per-seat snag timers, per-hull water and its bailer), the beer-loss cooldown (`beer_loss_cooldown`), the empty-boat drift (`empty_drift_multiplier`) and `lake_seed`; `new_attempt()` (draws `lake_seed`; a shore reset keeps it), `reset()`, `stroke(seat, hold_time, backwards)`, `step(delta, boat_at) -> Motion` (takes jumpers off the boat), `set_progress()`, `arrive()`, `beer_overboard(hull, at)`, `guy_overboard(seat, at)`, `beast_grab(hull, from, spit_distance)` (the hull's guy spat out toward the shore, beerless; emits `guy_grabbed`), `pickup(seat)`, `shore_touched()` (resets only in CROSSING; a washed-up swimmer re-boards on his own in LAUNCH), `shove()`/`take_shove()`, `push()`/`take_push()` (sustained), `set_extra_drift()`, `stop()`/`take_stop()`, `snag(hull, s)`, `force_break(hull)`, `water_aboard(hull, amount)`, `can_paddle(seat)`; the play log (T70): every action, rescue step, hazard effect, antic, break, bar change, shore reset and win writes a `PlayLog` line with a stable id (`action.stroke`, `rescue.jumped`, `hazard.snag`, `guy.antic`, `run.won`, ...), a snapshot at each attempt start and shore reset; one log run per process, each `new_attempt()` (R, the touch hold, autoplay's reset) is attempt n+1 of it (`attempt`, T83); `snapshot()` and `block_reason()` feed the log's hooks; the dev menu's hooks (T72): `debug_beer_left/right()`, `debug_force_antic(seat, kind)`, `debug_set_drunkenness/stamina(seat, level)`, `debug_force_break(seat)`, `debug_snag(seat)`, `debug_water(side)`, `debug_win()` and `debug_inspect()`; the rule classes Antics, Stamina, Drunkenness and Rescue each have a `debug_inspect()` | `scripts/autoload/run_state.gd` | the boat scene calls `stroke`/`step` and applies the `Motion`; the lake sets progress, calls `arrive`, `pickup` and `shore_touched`; screens read it; emits `run_reset`, `shore_reset_done`, `stroke_landed`, `stroke_fumbled`, `guy_jumped`, `guy_fell_in`, `guy_picked_up`, `guy_grabbed`, `puked`, `snagged`, `beer_overboard_started` |
| `PlayLog` (autoload, Godot Director) | the play log of each run: `log_event(category, event, text, data)`, `begin_run()`, `log_snapshot()`, `start_capture()` for tests; the base is framework-owned (`tools/play_log_base.gd`), the game's seed `tools/play_log.gd` holds the hooks (`_build_snapshot()`, `progress_block_reason()`, `view_changed`) | `tools/play_log.gd` | registered first in project.godot; the hooks read `RunState.snapshot()` and `RunState.block_reason()`; `lake.gd` logs the `lake` and `win` views and the reset; tests capture lines with `start_capture()` (T70) |
| `DevMenu` (autoload, Godot Director) | the in-game dev menu on F12 in debug builds: tabs per registered system with its `debug_*` actions and `debug_inspect()`, and a Log tab; the base is framework-owned (`tools/dev_menu_base.gd`), the game's seed `tools/dev_menu.gd` registers the systems | `tools/dev_menu.gd` | registered last in project.godot; tabs Run (RunState: its debug_* hooks, new_attempt, shore_touched, pickup), Rescue, Antics (interrupt), Stamina (call_break), Drunkenness (relieve) and Log (T72); Beast (registered by `lake.gd` per lake, T84); absent in release builds |
| `Audio` (autoload) | the Sfx player pool, `play(name, pitch_scale)`, loops for hazards (`play_loop`/`stop_loop`, capped), the master volume (held here until a settings system exists), and the mapping of `RunState` signals (stroke landed/fumbled, jump, pickup, beer overboard) to sounds; buses in `assets/audio/default_bus_layout.tres` | `scripts/autoload/audio.gd` | listens to `RunState`; inert during the check; sounds generated by `tools/gen_audio.gd` |
| `Lines` (Resource) | the Swedish lines per telegraph kind (guy wind-ups and hazards) and `pick(kind, rng)`; `data/lines.tres` is the human's to edit | `scripts/resources/lines.gd` | `RunState` owns it (`pick_line`, `telegraph(kind, hull)` → signal `telegraphed`); hazards call `_telegraph` at their warning |
| `Names` (Resource) | the crew's nicknames and `pick_four(rng)`; `data/names.tres` is the human's to edit | `scripts/resources/names.gd` | `RunState` owns it (`guy_names` drawn at `new_attempt()`, kept by a shore reset, `name_of(seat)`); the HUD and the win title show them |
| `Paddling` (rule class) | stroke events (seat, hold time, backwards: thrust × `reverse_thrust_fraction` negated, turn negated) and a timestep into thrust, signed turn (+ = right) and drift toward the shore; seats enum matching RunState; tuning in `BoatTuning` (`scripts/resources/boat_tuning.gd`, `data/boat_tuning.tres`) | `scripts/systems/paddling.gd` | pure: receives `BoatTuning`; `RunState` owns the instance; `catamaran.gd` applies its `Motion` with the boat feel values in `BoatTuning` (thrust_scale, turn_scale, damping) |
| `Drunkenness` (rule class) | one 0..1 level per seat, `relieve(seat, amount)` for the puke and pee drops; drink rate by state (break, water, no beer); stroke delay and miss chance per level (`Curve`s); seeded miss roll; seeing double at `double_level` (T89): a per-seat cycle of wind-up, `double_duration` with every stroke's direction flipped, `double_cooldown` off, paused while he is in the water (`reversed`, `double_winding_up`); no minigame; tuning in `DrinkTuning` (`scripts/resources/drink_tuning.gd`, `data/drink_tuning.tres`) | `scripts/systems/drunkenness.gd` | pure: receives `DrinkTuning` and an RNG; `RunState` owns the instance and steps it |
| `Antics` (rule class) | per seat IDLE → WINDING_UP (`windup_time`) → STANDING (rocks the boat) \| PADDLE_DROPPED \| NODDED_OFF \| PEEING (knocked in by a shove ≥ `pee_fall_shove`) \| PUKING (started by `RunState` at `puke_level`, never drawn) → RECOVERING; `just_finished` for the step an antic ran its course; one antic per `antic_thresholds` crossing per attempt, drawn from the caller's RNG; `can_stroke`, `rock`, `knocked_in`, `interrupt`; tunables on `DrinkTuning` | `scripts/systems/antics.gd` | pure; `RunState` owns the instance, steps it per guy aboard, adds the rock to the Motion's turn and applies the knock-in via `guy_overboard`; `catamaran.gd` shows each antic |
| `Stamina` (rule class) | one 0..1 level per seat; burn per stroke power, recovery; per-seat PADDLING → WARNING → BREAK machine (`can_stroke`, `time_left`, `call_break` for hazards); random breaks from the caller's RNG; tuning in `StaminaTuning` (`scripts/resources/stamina_tuning.gd`, `data/stamina_tuning.tres`) | `scripts/systems/stamina.gd` | pure: receives `StaminaTuning` and an RNG; `RunState` owns the instance and steps it; `catamaran.gd` reads its state for the telegraphs |
| `Hazards` (rule class) | hazard placement: the lake in bands of `band_length`, each drawing `density_curve` hazards from the weighted `HazardSpec` list (`weight`, `first_band`), objects placed within `lake_width` with their `half_width` footprint clear of the guaranteed path (skipped when no room), event triggers (`HazardSpec.is_event`) placed on it; the path is a corridor of `path_width` that never enters the beast's bounds or its warning band whose centre random-walks by at most `path_wander` per band from the launch to the island (`path_center`, `corridor_x(tuning, seed, y)`), all from one seed (`plan(tuning, seed)`; per band `plan_band(tuning, seed, band, generation)`, kept for the path and future events; objects are fixed and never refill) | `scripts/systems/hazards.gd` | pure; `lake.gd` spawns the planned scenes under its `Hazards` node from `RunState.lake_seed`; hazard scenes (`scripts/hazards/*.gd`) get `tuning` (LakeTuning) and `spec` from the lake and call `RunState`'s effect methods on contact |
| `GeeseFlock` (rule class) | one flock: NESTING → HISSING (warning; the charge always follows) → CHARGING (pecks at an interval while in range, each a beer overboard on the nearest hull) → RETURNING → cooldown; its position and timers; tunables `geese_*` on `LakeTuning` | `scripts/systems/geese.gd` | pure; `scripts/hazards/geese.gd` steps it with the boat's position and applies pecks via `RunState.beer_overboard` |
| `MotorboatWake` (rule class) | one wake event: WAITING → CROSSING (a motorboat across the lake ahead of the trigger, trailing a V of two fronts, `Front.SHORE` and `Front.ISLAND`, half-lines from its position back at ±`wake_angle` from its travel) → ROLLING (the apex parked, each front spreading along its normal at `wake_speed` until a trigger radius past the trigger) → SPENT; the first front to cross a boat behind the apex gives the event's one hit: head-on into it (within `wake_head_on_angle` of its normal) nothing, and the other front may still hit, so a boat can cross the V at an angle; broadside a beer overboard on the high hull, a sideways shove and water aboard (T88); apart from the wave, the motorboat's hull rams a boat within `wake_hull_radius` once per event, read with `take_ram()`: a beer overboard on the hull facing it and a shove of `wake_ram_shove` along its travel (T91); tunables `wake_*` on `LakeTuning` | `scripts/systems/wake.gd` | pure; `scripts/hazards/wake.gd` steps it with the boat's position and heading and applies the hit via `RunState` |
| `WindGust` (rule class) | one gust event: WAITING → WARNING (`gust_warning`, ripples) → BLOWING (a sideways push of `gust_shove` for `gust_time`, direction from the trigger's side) → SPENT | `scripts/systems/gust.gd` | pure; `scripts/hazards/gust.gd` steps it and feeds `RunState.push()` |
| `Headwind` (rule class) | one harsh-wind stretch (`headwind_length` tall, lake wide) where the drift is stronger by `headwind_force` | `scripts/systems/headwind.gd` | pure; `scripts/hazards/headwind.gd` calls `RunState.set_extra_drift()` while the boat is inside |
| `CrossCurrent` (rule class) | one fixed lake-wide stripe of `stripe_length` pushing sideways by `stripe_force` (direction from its side of the lake) | `scripts/systems/cross_current.gd` | pure; `scripts/hazards/cross_current.gd` feeds `RunState.push()` while the boat is inside |
| `JetSki` (rule class) | one jet-ski event: WAITING → WARNING (`jetski_warning`) → CROSSING (a diagonal from the lake edge at the trigger's y through a point `jetski_lead` ahead of the boat, at `jetski_speed`) → SPENT; one hit within `jetski_hit_radius`: a shove of `jetski_shove` along its travel and a beer overboard on the hull it came from | `scripts/systems/jetski.gd` | pure; `scripts/hazards/jetski.gd` steps it and applies the hit via `RunState` |
| `Beast` (rule class) | the one Loch-Brez beast per attempt: SUBMERGED → CIRCLING (`beast_warning`) → RAMMING (closes at `beast_speed` from the side away from the lake's middle; a hit shoves by `beast_shove` toward the middle and grabs the hull's guy) → DIVING; rises when the boat is out of bounds (`beast_edge_margin`, `beast_beyond_island`) until it is back, and in open water only if `beast_random_rate` is above zero (it is 0: never a random encounter); a puke heard in open water rolls `beast_puke_chance` (`hear_puke`); a warning band of `beast_warn_margin` inside the bounds (`near_bounds`, `check_approach` once per approach → telegraph `edge`); `rise_cause` names why it rose; `debug_inspect()` for the dev menu's Beast tab, which `lake.gd` registers on spawn and replaces per lake (T84) | `scripts/systems/beast.gd` | pure; `scripts/hazards/beast.gd` (spawned once per lake by `lake.gd`, not planned) steps it with its own RNG (`Beast.rng_for(RunState.lake_seed)`, so the same lake gives the same visits) and applies the hit via `RunState.shove` and `RunState.beast_grab`; idle on WON |
| `Stats` (rule class) | the crossing's counters (T86): strokes landed, fumbles, reverse strokes, beers overboard and rescued, jumps, falls, beast grabs, pickups, pukes, pisses, naps, stand-ups, paddles dropped, breaks, snags, water bailed, shoves, stops, pushes, shore resets, drunkenness gained and the drunkest level; `count(event, data)` maps the play log's event ids to them, `add_drunk()`, `beers_drunk(level_per_beer)` (gained ÷ `DrinkTuning.level_per_beer`, rounded), `times_in_water()`, `rows(elapsed, level_per_beer)` in a stable order with zero rows kept, `format_time()` | `scripts/systems/stats.gd` | pure; `RunState` owns the instance, feeds it from `_log` and the drink step, resets it at `new_attempt()` (a shore reset keeps it); `win_cutscene.gd` fills `%Stats` from `rows()` |
| `Rescue` (rule class) | the guys in the water and their beers: `LooseBeer` records (a beer bobbing away while its guy winds up for `jump_delay`), `Swimmer` records (seat, position, beer, SWIMMING → FLOATING, has_beer; `kind(swim_back_delay)` names what he is doing, `KIND_FETCH/DRINK/FELL/BACK/WASHED_UP`, read by the bubbles and the HUD, T80), who jumps for a beer on a hull, `step(delta, current, tuning, boat_at) -> seats that jumped`, `winding_up`, `within_reach`, `pickup`; no limit on swimmers; after `swim_back_delay` a floating guy swims slowly back toward the boat (`swim_back_speed`); nothing loose drifts past the shoreline (`BoatTuning.shore_margin`), a floating guy there is `washed_up`; `guy_overboard` for beerless falls (pee antic; a guy winding up swims for his beer instead) | `scripts/systems/rescue.gd` | pure; `RunState` owns the instance; `lake.gd` draws swimmers and beers, picks up within `BoatTuning.pickup_radius`, and reports shore contact |
