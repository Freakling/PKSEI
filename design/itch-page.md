## Description

**PKSEI** stands for *Paddla Kanot Som En Idiot*: Swedish for "paddle a canoe like an idiot". It is a rage game, and it is also a tech demo.

### The game

Four drunk guys have lashed two aluminium canoes into a catamaran and need to reach an island on the far side of a huge lake. You control all four of them at once. The lake throws logs, geese, wakes, gusts, a jet-ski idiot and, if you stray too far, the Loch-Brez beast at them, and the guys keep drinking, so the boat gets harder to control the longer the crossing takes. Nobody dies. A bad stretch just drifts you back toward the shore, drunker than before.

Think *Getting Over It*, but on water, with three friends who are no help at all.

**Controls (keyboard):** each guy has two keys, hold to wind up, release to stroke.

- Left hull: **Q / A** front seat (forward / reverse), **W / S** back seat
- Right hull: **I / K** front seat, **O / L** back seat

Front seats are the engine, back seats the rudder. A stroke on the left turns you right, like an outboard. Paddle too hard and a guy needs a break; let him get too drunk and he does something stupid. Beer overboard means someone jumps in after it, and you'll have to go back for him.

Touch controls for phones are on the way.

### The tech demo

PKSEI is built with [Godot Director](https://github.com/Freakling/Godot-Director), a workflow where a human directs the game design and an AI builds it: every design call comes to the designer as options, every change is proved by one project check (tests, scene loads, screen-script rules), and the records (design document, decisions, task list) stay the single source of truth. The whole game so far, code, generated pixel art and generated sound, was built this way in two days. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Freakling/PKSEI).

Everything you see is a placeholder being tuned: the numbers, the art, the sounds. Comments on what feels unfair, funny or broken are exactly what this page is for.

### Where it comes from

Every year the author and a few friends go out camping the way the game does: canoes, a lake, an island, too much beer. The game is the trip with the physics turned up.
